## Soul 3.3.0

**New: avatar details**
- On anyone's profile, click any avatar they've worn (or "details ›" on their current avatar) to see:
  name, creator, public/private, PC/Quest support, performance rating, upload and update dates,
  style, content warnings, tags, the **avatar ID**, and how many times they wore it.
- Public avatars: **Wear this** or ★ add to your favorites right from there.
- Private avatars show what VRChat allows (name, creator, dates).

**Cleaner look**
- Sidebar grouped into Social / Explore.
- Friend cards show full names; star, message and poke buttons appear when you hover.
- Profile panel: one tidy row (Message, Poke, Join, ★) with everything else under ⋯.
- Smaller, calmer section headings and spacing throughout.
